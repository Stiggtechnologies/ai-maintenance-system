# Model

Inputs are contemporaneous shaft speed, a declared forcing order, a measured dominant spectral peak, and evidenced bounds for modal mass and stiffness.

Outputs are shaft frequency, forcing frequency, estimated natural frequency, and the percentage separation of the measured peak from the two references. A classification is emitted only when an approved tolerance and its source are present.

The model deliberately does not use vibration amplitude as an alarm threshold. A frequency match is evidence supporting a hypothesis, not confirmation of resonance. Field confirmation can include a qualified impact test, run-up/coast-down assessment, or an approved equivalent.
