# Conventions

- Unit system: SI.
- Shaft speed: revolutions per minute, averaged over the spectrum window.
- Frequency: hertz.
- Forcing order: positive dimensionless multiple of shaft frequency.
- Vibration basis: RMS for amplitude metadata; amplitude is not consumed by this screen.
- Rotation: positive magnitude; direction-sensitive modes are excluded.
- Time basis: operating time.
- Sensor axis and orientation: declared in canonical measurement evidence.
