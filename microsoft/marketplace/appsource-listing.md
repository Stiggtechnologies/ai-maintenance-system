# AppSource Listing Draft

## Offer Name

SyncAI Reliability Engineering Copilot

## Short Description

Reliability copilot for RCA, FRACAS, FMEA, RAM calculations, and executive
reliability reporting.

## Long Description

SyncAI Reliability Engineering Copilot helps asset-intensive organizations turn
maintenance records, failure histories, reliability documents, and work-order
exports into traceable reliability engineering decisions.

It supports reliability engineering workflows including root cause analysis
(RCA), FRACAS/DCACAS case support, FMEA/FMECA worksheets, RCM and PM
optimization, MTBF/MTTR/availability calculations, bad actor analysis, asset
criticality, and executive reliability reporting.

SyncAI is designed as a governed decision-support system. It separates evidence,
assumptions, calculations, and recommendations, and routes high-risk safety,
environmental, regulatory, OEM-limit, and production-critical changes through
human engineering approval.

## Primary Use Cases

- Analyze work-order exports and failure histories.
- Create RCA starter packs from failure evidence.
- Create and manage FRACAS case drafts.
- Build FMEA/FMECA worksheets.
- Calculate MTBF, MTTR, failure rate, reliability, and availability.
- Identify bad actors by downtime, failure count, cost, or risk.
- Create plant-manager and executive reliability briefs.
- Convert reliability analysis into corrective action plans.

## Differentiators

- Reliability-engineering workflow focus, not generic chat.
- Deterministic calculation tools for core RAM metrics.
- Structured RCA, FRACAS, FMEA, RCM, and executive workflows.
- Microsoft 365 and Teams distribution path.
- Human-in-the-loop governance for high-risk decisions.
- Designed to work with existing CMMS/EAM/APM systems instead of replacing
  them.

## What Not To Claim

Do not claim:

- Guaranteed downtime reduction.
- Replacement of licensed professional engineers.
- Certified ISO, IEC, SAE, API, or regulatory compliance.
- Autonomous approval of safety-critical or regulatory actions.
- Native integration with a CMMS/EAM/APM unless that connector is deployed.

Allowed positioning:

> Built on recognized reliability engineering practices and configurable to
> customer standards, work processes, and approval requirements.
