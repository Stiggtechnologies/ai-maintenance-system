# Design Partner Pilot SOW Template

Use this for the first paid SyncAI Reliability Engineering Copilot design
partners. Default commercial wedge: CAD $7,500, 14 days, one bounded asset
class/system/problem area, exported data only.

## Project

SyncAI Reliability Engineering Copilot paid pilot.

## Objective

Help the customer evaluate whether SyncAI can turn maintenance records, failure
history, reliability documents, and work-order exports into traceable
reliability engineering outputs such as RCA drafts, FRACAS cases, bad actor
analysis, RAM calculations, PM optimization opportunities, and executive
reliability briefs.

The objective is not a full enterprise integration. The objective is to prove
that a bounded export can produce a useful reliability decision package.

## Scope

Included:

- One target site, line, system, or asset class.
- One defined historical data period, preferably 12-24 months.
- Customer-provided documents and exports.
- Reliability Copilot analysis.
- RCA/FRACAS/FMEA/PM optimization outputs as agreed in kickoff.
- One guided reliability-ready asset onboarding package.
- One executive reliability summary.
- One review meeting.

Excluded unless separately agreed:

- Live CMMS integration.
- Production control-system integration.
- Safety-critical approval.
- Regulatory interpretation.
- Engineering sign-off.
- Guaranteed downtime or cost reduction.
- Full enterprise rollout.

## Deliverables

- Data intake and quality summary.
- Bad actor analysis.
- MTBF / MTTR / availability calculations where data supports them.
- Reliability-ready asset onboarding package.
- RCA starter pack or FRACAS case draft.
- PM optimization opportunity list.
- Corrective action register or action plan.
- Executive reliability brief.
- Pilot findings and recommended next step.

## Customer Responsibilities

- Provide source documents and exports described in
  `docs/paid-pilot-data-request.md`.
- Confirm rights to use uploaded documents.
- Provide subject-matter expert access for validation.
- Identify approval owners for high-risk recommendations.
- Review outputs before operational use.

## SyncAI Responsibilities

- Configure pilot workspace.
- Run reliability analysis using SyncAI Copilot.
- Separate evidence, assumptions, calculations, and recommendations.
- Identify missing data and validation needs.
- Provide review-ready outputs.

## Decision Support Boundary

SyncAI outputs are decision support. The customer remains responsible for
engineering approval, field validation, safety, regulatory compliance, OEM-limit
changes, operating-envelope changes, and implementation decisions.

## Duration

Default pilot duration: 14 days.

Optional expanded pilot duration: 2 to 4 weeks.

## Commercial Terms

Pilot fee: CAD $7,500 for the first design-partner pilot.

Payment schedule: due at kickoff unless separately agreed.

Subscription conversion option: pilot fee can be credited toward a larger paid
pilot, implementation, or annual subscription if agreed within 30 days of pilot
completion.

## Success Criteria

- Customer receives a useful reliability analysis package.
- Customer identifies at least one actionable reliability opportunity.
- Customer can see how SyncAI fits existing maintenance/reliability workflows.
- Customer agrees whether to proceed to implementation or subscription.

## Minimum Data Package

The customer may de-identify data. Minimum useful package:

- 12-24 months of work orders or notifications.
- Asset register or tag list for the selected area/system.
- Current PM/task list if available.
- Existing RCA/FMEA/inspection/condition-monitoring/OEM excerpts if available
  and approved for use.
