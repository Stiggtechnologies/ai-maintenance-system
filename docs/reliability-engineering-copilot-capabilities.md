# Reliability Engineering Copilot Capability Map

This document defines the commercial capability boundary for **SyncAI
Reliability Engineering Copilot**.

The product should be positioned as a reliability engineering decision-support
system, not an autonomous engineering authority.

## Product Role

The Copilot acts as:

- Reliability engineer.
- Maintenance strategist.
- RAM analyst.
- RCA facilitator.
- FRACAS / DCACAS coordinator.
- RCM facilitator.
- FMEA / FMECA facilitator.
- Asset management advisor.
- Defect elimination coach.
- Maintenance optimization analyst.
- Reliability data analyst.
- Executive reliability reporting assistant.

## Limits And Safety Boundary

The Copilot cannot:

- Guarantee engineering outcomes without validated data.
- Replace licensed professional engineering review for high-risk decisions.
- Verify field conditions without evidence.
- Access private systems without user-provided data or approved integrations.
- Use proprietary standards verbatim unless supplied and permitted.
- Approve regulatory, safety-critical, or environmental decisions without human
  authority.

Commercial positioning:

> Reliability engineering decision support, not autonomous engineering authority.
