## Evidence into governed engineering decisions

SyncAI helps industrial asset teams turn operational records and engineering
evidence into traceable reliability decisions. It connects asset and component
context, evidence-linked engineering knowledge, deterministic calculations
where inputs support them, and AI-assisted analysis in a governed workspace.

Teams can use SyncAI to:

- assess vibration, oil-analysis, thermography, motor-current, and
  process-condition evidence with its operating context;
- run governed censored life-data and selected deterministic reliability and
  physics-of-failure calculations when the required evidence and approved
  limits exist;
- investigate equipment issues and evidence gaps;
- structure reliability, failure, inspection, and engineering cases;
- distinguish supported findings, hypotheses, and missing evidence;
- review recommendations through defined human approval boundaries; and
- preserve the evidence and rationale behind a decision.

## Built for human authority

SyncAI is decision support. It does not replace qualified engineering
judgment, operating authority, legal requirements, OEM limits, or site
procedures. Consequential recommendations remain subject to defined human
review and approval. The availability of individual capabilities depends on
the configured data, purchased scope, deployment, and applicable agreement.

## What a rollout establishes

A controlled rollout defines the organization and user scope, source data,
asset context, enabled workflows, integration boundaries, evidence and
approval responsibilities, support model, retention, and acceptance criteria.
The team then validates the experience against customer-approved cases before
expanding scope.

SyncAI can begin with customer-provided exports. Read-only CMMS connector
patterns cover generic HTTPS JSON, SAP PM, IBM Maximo, and Oracle EAM for
scoped validation. Production CMMS and historian connections remain dependent
on the customer environment, approved access, mapping, deployment
configuration, and acceptance testing.

## Important boundaries

SyncAI does not guarantee savings, uptime improvement, failure avoidance,
accuracy, or ROI. Production identity, connectors, deployment topology,
availability commitments, security representations, and data-processing terms
are confirmed for each purchased scope. Public evaluation access is not an
authorization to submit confidential production information or change
production systems.
