# SyncAI — from first evidence to enterprise operating capability

**Customer-facing source for accessible PDF production. Controlled draft;
commercial terms and Marketplace availability are confirmed only by the
applicable private offer and signed agreement.**

## One governed path to value

SyncAI helps industrial asset, reliability and maintenance teams turn operating
questions into evidence-backed, human-governed decisions—and carry accepted
decisions through verification and learning.

Customers can engage through three bounded professional-service offers that
support the SyncAI SaaS platform:

1. **Reliability Intelligence Assessment** — establish what current evidence
   proves, identify material gaps and prioritize a governed 90-day action plan.
2. **Industrial Decision Proof of Concept** — prove SyncAI on a bounded
   decision, fleet or site using agreed controls, acceptance criteria and stop
   criteria.
3. **SyncAI Implementation and Scale** — deploy approved workflows,
   integrations and operating practices with a bounded Forward-Deployed
   Engineering workstream.

Each stage ends with a customer-owned choice: proceed, change the scope, hold,
or stop. There is no automatic expansion.

## Forward-Deployed Engineering, productized

SyncAI Forward-Deployed Engineering brings named practitioners into the
implementation alongside the customer's product, reliability, maintenance,
engineering, data and security owners. The team connects authorized data,
configures governed workflows and specialist roles, proves acceptance criteria,
supports adoption and transfers the operating runbook.

The engagement is defined by outcomes and acceptance evidence—not by supplying
unbounded labor. Exact scope, systems, sites, milestones, deliverables and
commercial terms are established in the customer order.

## Evidence and authority remain visible

SyncAI keeps facts, assumptions, recommendations, decisions, approvals,
verification obligations, observed value and lessons distinct. Evidence retains
its provenance and verification status. Value hypotheses remain hypotheses
until the agreed method and evidence verify them.

The customer retains engineering, investment, operating, maintenance, safety,
regulatory and risk-acceptance authority. SyncAI and its FDE team do not become
the engineer of record, operate equipment, change operating limits, approve
customer recommendations or issue customer work.

## What accepted implementation looks like

An accepted production implementation has:

- a named customer sponsor and accountable decision owners;
- an approved tenant, role, data and authority boundary;
- governed source integrations with provenance and tenant isolation;
- selected workflows and specialist roles configured for the agreed scope;
- measurable acceptance and verification methods;
- a customer-owned operating runbook, support route and handover record; and
- an evidence pack for the next go, change, hold or stop decision.

## Commercial path

Professional services are delivered virtually through customer-specific
Microsoft Marketplace private offers when available. The recurring SyncAI SaaS
subscription remains the platform anchor. The private offer and signed agreement
control price, markets, schedule, scope, data handling, responsibilities,
acceptance and exclusions.

SyncAI does not guarantee savings, uptime, accuracy, failure avoidance or return
on investment. Results depend on authorized data, customer decisions, operating
conditions, implementation and verified observation.
