## Commercial architecture

SyncAI uses one recurring SaaS offer as the platform anchor and three bounded,
transactable professional-service offers alongside it:

| Offer                                | Microsoft category | Customer outcome                                                                                                  | Exit                                                                                                 |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Reliability Intelligence Assessment  | Assessment         | Establish what the available evidence proves, identify material gaps and prioritize a governed 90-day action plan | Customer records proceed, change, hold or stop for a proof of concept                                |
| Industrial Decision Proof of Concept | Proof of concept   | Prove SyncAI on a bounded decision, fleet or site against agreed evidence, controls and acceptance criteria       | Customer records proceed, change, hold or stop for production deployment                             |
| SyncAI Implementation and Scale      | Implementation     | Operationalize approved data, governed workflows and customer ownership in production                             | Customer records proceed, change, hold or stop for the next fleet, site, network or enterprise scope |

These are not subscription tiers and do not replace the SaaS offer. Microsoft
requires a professional service supporting third-party software to reference
that software's Marketplace listing. The professional-service submissions stay
blocked until the SyncAI SaaS listing is published and its listing URL is
recorded.

Microsoft currently permits professional services only through private offers,
requires virtual delivery, and does not expose transactable professional-service
listings through storefront search. Each customer therefore receives a direct
private-offer link and an approved order/SOW. Microsoft handles the Marketplace
transaction; SyncAI remains responsible for service fulfillment.

## The value ladder and operating loop

The commercial ladder and the product decision loop are deliberately separate:

```text
First Decision
    ↓
RIA when the readiness/evidence baseline is not already proven
    ↓
Bounded proof of concept
    ↓
Production fleet or site
    ↓
Multi-site or network
    ↓
Enterprise standard

At every scope:
Ask → Understand → Save → Prove → Recommend → Decide → Approve
    → Verify → Collaborate → Learn
```

RIA is not a mandatory tollgate. It may be bypassed only when the customer and
SyncAI can evidence a validated baseline and counterfactual, named sponsor and
decision owners, authorized data, agreed acceptance and stop criteria, and an
approved governance/security boundary.

No usage threshold, enthusiasm signal, account activity, service milestone or
AI recommendation advances a customer up the ladder. Every scope increase
requires a named customer authority to record a canonical Expansion Decision of
**go, change, hold or stop**, including unresolved risks and verification
obligations.

## Forward-Deployed Engineering

Forward-Deployed Engineering (FDE) is the principal delivery workstream inside
**SyncAI Implementation and Scale**. It is not a fourth adoption rung, a new
authority model, or open-ended staff augmentation.

A bounded FDE engagement places named SyncAI practitioners alongside the
customer's product, reliability, maintenance, engineering, data and security
owners—virtually for this Marketplace offer—to:

- define the approved solution and authority boundary;
- connect only authorized sources and preserve provenance;
- configure tenant roles, selected workflows and specialist roles;
- establish acceptance, verification and value-measurement methods;
- support adoption, training and operating-runbook completion; and
- prepare the evidence pack for the next human Expansion Decision.

Each FDE statement of work must identify scope, named owners, approved
environments and sources, milestones, deliverables, acceptance evidence,
prohibited actions, escalation routes, data handling, rollback/continuity,
commercial term and exit conditions. Time spent or people assigned is not, by
itself, an accepted outcome.

### Authority boundary

The customer always retains:

- engineering and engineer-of-record authority;
- investment and risk-acceptance authority;
- operating, maintenance and work-execution authority;
- safety, regulatory and environmental accountability; and
- production credential, change and release authority.

SyncAI and its FDE practitioners do not approve customer recommendations,
accept customer risk, change operating limits or protection settings, issue or
close customer work, operate or isolate equipment, or use data and credentials
outside the authorized scope. Product controls and an SOW reinforce this
boundary; neither can transfer an authority the customer did not explicitly and
lawfully delegate.

## Canonical product handoff

Services create no parallel sales evidence, decision, approval, value or audit
store. Where the customer authorizes persistence in SyncAI, the engagement
reuses:

- `evidence_items` for source-backed facts and provenance;
- `recommendations` for advisory actions and rationale;
- `decisions` and `approvals` for customer determinations and authority;
- `verification_obligations` for promised follow-through;
- `value_metrics` for baselines, observations and verified realization;
- `learning_events` for accepted lessons; and
- `audit_events` for the governed activity trail.

The approved order, Marketplace record and customer contract remain the
commercial systems of record. SyncAI stores only the bounded delivery context
that the customer has authorized and that the product needs to operate.

## Offer-specific gates

### Reliability Intelligence Assessment

- Standard commercial reference: **US$35,000 fixed**, normally **6–8 weeks**;
  final terms remain the approved proposal/SOW and private offer.
- Inputs: authorized exports, named sponsor, agreed questions and approved data
  handling. No production credentials or installation are assumed.
- Required outputs: evidence/data-readiness inventory, supported and unsupported
  findings, prioritized decision/opportunity register, value hypotheses with
  assumptions, 90-day action plan and Expansion Decision pack.
- Acceptance requires the customer to confirm source scope, material gaps,
  decision ownership, verification method and the go/change/hold/stop outcome.

### Industrial Decision Proof of Concept

- Bounded to agreed decisions, data, tenant scope, acceptance criteria and stop
  criteria; normally no more than 90 days unless the order states otherwise.
- Required outputs: configured proof environment, provenance map, governed
  representative decision cases, control-gap log, acceptance/value scorecard,
  operating runbook and production Expansion Decision pack.
- A value hypothesis is never reported as realized value. Every observation
  names its baseline, method, window, evidence and verification status.
- The proof does not authorize production write-back or automatically expand a
  subscription.

### SyncAI Implementation and Scale

- Begins only after an approved production Expansion Decision and signed scope,
  acceptance plan, owner map, data/environment authorization and release
  controls.
- Required outputs: approved production boundary, governed integrations and
  workflows, control evidence, acceptance scorecard, operating runbook,
  training/handover evidence and the next Expansion Decision pack.
- Exact systems, fleets, sites, specialist roles, milestones and support are
  contract-defined; the offer name implies none of them by itself.
- FDE remains bounded and virtual under this Marketplace offer.

## Partner Center operator map

Create three separate **Professional service** offers. Use the exact listing
copy, proposed IDs, keywords, plan templates and controlled statuses in the
machine-readable record. Before submission:

1. Record the published SyncAI SaaS Marketplace listing URL on each offer's
   Properties page.
2. Obtain commercial/legal/tax approval for every immutable ID, market, plan
   duration, list-plan price, billing frequency and private-offer pricing rule.
3. Complete accessibility tagging review and brand/legal/claims approval for
   [`../../output/pdf/syncai-professional-services-and-fde.pdf`](../../output/pdf/syncai-professional-services-and-fde.pdf),
   then upload it with a compliant square logo and at least one 1280×720
   promotional image for each offer. Visual and text-extraction QA have passed;
   the PDF is not yet represented as accessibility-approved or upload-approved.
4. Verify two non-public Marketplace contacts and a stable HTTPS privacy link.
5. Submit each offer and retain a redacted Partner Center export or screenshot.
6. After publication, create a customer-specific private offer with absolute
   pricing and the approved terms. Microsoft does not email the customer; send
   the generated link through the controlled sales process.
7. Witness customer acceptance and purchase separately from service delivery.
   Retain only redacted evidence and never commit customer billing data.

Professional-service publication does not prove a successful private-offer
purchase, and a purchase does not prove service acceptance or business value.
Those are distinct evidence gates.
