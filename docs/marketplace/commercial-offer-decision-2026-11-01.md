# SyncAI Microsoft commercial offer decision — 1 November 2026

## Decision

The core SyncAI Microsoft SaaS offer will use **flat-rate, site-and-outcome
pricing**. The current per-user Starter, Professional, and Enterprise drafts
must not be published.

This is a controlled operating decision for preview preparation. It is not an
assertion that the offer is certified, production-commerce ready, or safe to
activate. Public activation remains blocked by the release gates below.

## Why this is the right architecture

SyncAI is sold to improve governed reliability decisions and reduce the
recurrence, duration, and economic impact of unplanned downtime. A seat is not
the economic unit the customer is buying. A site, network, or enterprise scope
is.

The Microsoft choice is also irreversible after publication: all plans in one
SaaS offer must share one pricing model, and the pricing model cannot be changed
after publication. Metered billing is available only to flat-rate SaaS plans.
The current per-user draft would therefore lock the core platform away from the
metering system already implemented in the repository.

The present one-seat Starter draft has only US$1,068 of annual contract value.
That cannot support material onboarding, security review, customer success, or
reliability-engineering support. It is a software experiment, not the core
commercial architecture.

## Controlled plan structure

| Plan | Visibility | Scope | Annual contract value | Included governed AI decisions | Launch behavior |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Site Reliability | Public | One site, up to 25 named users | US$60,000 | 20 per month | Hard stop; upgrade or approved private offer |
| Reliability Network | Private | Up to five sites and 100 named users | US$180,000 | 60 per month | Hard stop; customer-authorized expansion |
| Enterprise Reliability | Private | Up to 15 sites and 250 named users | US$360,000 | 120 per month | Hard stop; custom scope through approved order |

Each plan uses a one-year contract with equal monthly payments. Implementation,
integration, custom residency, forward-deployed engineering, and outcome proof
work are separate contracted services. The plans do not guarantee uptime,
savings, avoided failures, or return on investment.

There is no automatic metered overage at launch. Flat-rate pricing preserves
that future option, but SyncAI will not activate a meter until the commercial
unit, immutable dimension, included quantity, price, entitlement binding, and
live Microsoft witness are approved.

## Entry products stay separate from the platform price

The outcome-led entry remains the **Unplanned Downtime Reduction Proof**,
delivered as the existing Reliability Intelligence Assessment or Industrial
Decision Proof of Concept depending on customer readiness. The controlled
reference price is US$35,000 for a normally six-to-eight-week bounded scope.
The exit is a customer-owned go, change, hold, or stop decision.

The narrow **Failure Investigation Agent** remains a separate acquisition
experiment. Causelink's current public Team anchor is US$6,250 per year for five
users, which confirms a low-ACV root-cause-analysis category. Putting that
agent inside the core SyncAI offer would lock the full platform to per-user
pricing. It can become a separate offer only after agent-specific entitlements,
self-serve onboarding, support burden, hard AI limits, and site-expansion
attribution are proven.

The pain-led acquisition messages—failure investigation, downtime recovery,
preventive-maintenance optimization, and maintenance-cost reduction—all enter
the same SyncAI organization and Decision Case platform. They are not separate
copies of the product.

## Unit-economics stress test

The model uses the 30 October 2026 Bank of Canada USD/CAD observation of 1.396,
Microsoft's 3% Marketplace service fee, a 10% provider-cost multiplier, and a
maximum C$9.2136 inference cost for one governed decision. That decision ceiling
assumes up to five model calls and 500,000 total tokens priced at the worst
allowed GPT-5.6 Terra output rate. It is deliberately conservative.

| Plan | Monthly revenue, CAD | Maximum inference cost | Marketplace fee | Other variable-cost reserve | Modeled gross margin |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Site Reliability | C$6,980.00 | C$184.27 | C$209.40 | C$1,100.00 | 78.60% |
| Reliability Network | C$20,940.00 | C$552.82 | C$628.20 | C$3,700.00 | 76.69% |
| Enterprise Reliability | C$41,880.00 | C$1,105.63 | C$1,256.40 | C$7,400.00 | 76.69% |

All three clear the controlled 75% variable-margin floor under planning
reserves. Network and Enterprise have only about 1.69 percentage points of
headroom. These figures therefore approve the architecture for controlled
preview preparation, not public activation. Actual hosting, storage,
observability, support, customer-success, and implementation effort must be
measured first.

## Value qualification

SyncAI will not quote a production plan until the customer validates the
addressable annual economic value. As a qualification range—not a guarantee or
gainshare commitment—the recurring price should normally represent 5% to 15%
of that customer-validated addressable value.

Every proof and production business case must keep projected value separate
from verified value and record:

1. baseline downtime events, hours, recovery duration, and maintenance cost;
2. the customer's approved economic value per hour or event;
3. the addressable scope and counterfactual;
4. proof and implementation investment;
5. observed result, attribution confidence, and material confounders;
6. independently verified result and payback; and
7. the customer-owned expansion decision.

## Route to US$100M ARR

The scale path is proof → site → network → enterprise standard. One portfolio
geometry is 401 Site customers, 300 Network customers, and 61 Enterprise
customers, producing US$100.02M ARR. This is arithmetic, not a forecast. The
operating system must measure proof-to-site conversion, site-to-network
expansion, enterprise standardization, retention, verified customer value,
gross margin, and support burden.

## Release gates

Public activation remains blocked until all of the following are evidenced:

1. Microsoft accepts and verifies the Canadian payment profile assignment.
2. The production Marketplace client secret is configured without exposing it.
3. The exact flat-rate hard-stop policies are deployed and approved.
4. The protected Reliability Engineer path uses the shared reservation and
   settlement boundary.
5. Two real preview purchases pass fulfillment, activation, lifecycle,
   entitlement, refusal, and certification tests.
6. Measured p50/p95 inference cost and complete cost-to-serve preserve at least
   75% gross margin.
7. Buyer-visible names, prices, limits, support, terms, and markets exactly
   match the controlled record.
8. Microsoft certification succeeds before go-live.
