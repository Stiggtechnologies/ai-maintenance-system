# Commercial Model & Billing

**Last updated:** August 19, 2026

Historical Starter/Professional subscription prices, uptime promises, free-trial entitlements and discount programs are retired unless they are explicitly restored in a current order form or public pricing page.

## Current entry paths

### Reliability Engineer evaluation
A bounded public/evaluation experience used to demonstrate governed reliability reasoning. It does not create production-system access or an enterprise SLA.

### Reliability Intelligence Assessment
The public standard offer is **US$35,000 fixed fee**, normally **6–8 weeks**, for one bounded operating domain within the published standard scope. The applicable proposal/SOW governs final scope, timing, data handling and commercial terms. A qualifying deployment may receive up to the published deployment credit when stated in the signed offer.

### Strategic pilot / enterprise deployment
Scoped commercially based on the workflows, data/integration boundary, security requirements, users/sites and support model. The signed order/SOW controls fees, billing schedule, availability commitments, credits, cancellation, retention and support.
